Upload your employee list instead of adding people one at a time. You will see exactly what would change before anything is saved.

  • The first row must be a header. It needs an id and a name; email and role are optional. Common spellings work (Employee ID, Full Name, Job Title), in any column order.
  • Anyone already on the roster (matched by id) is updated, not duplicated, and their recorded findings stay attached.
  • Any other column is ignored and shown to you as ignored, so a salary or a national id in the file is never stored.
